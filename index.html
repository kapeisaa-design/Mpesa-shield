      Learn before you lose

      Understanding common social-engineering techniques can help you make safer decisions.

      Never share your PIN

      Your PIN is private. Treat unexpected requests for it as a serious warning sign.

      Protect your OTP

      Never disclose verification codes to unexpected callers or messages.

      Question urgency

      Scammers may pressure you to act quickly before you have time to verify information.
